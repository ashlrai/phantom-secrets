# Commercial support boundary

Phantom's public repository uses community support. Public questions and bug
reports are handled as maintainer availability allows through the routes in
[SUPPORT.md](../SUPPORT.md). The repository does not promise priority response,
resolution time, uptime, or a contractual service level.

An organization may ask Ashlr AI to scope paid support around a named Phantom
evaluation or deployment. No commercial support entitlement exists until a
written agreement defines it.

## What a support agreement must define

At minimum, record:

- the organization, repositories, Phantom versions, platforms, and clients in
  scope;
- the support period, staffed hours, intake channel, and authorized contacts;
- response targets and whether they are targets or contractual commitments;
- severity definitions, required diagnostic evidence, and escalation owners;
- included advisory, integration, or incident-assistance work;
- exclusions, third-party/provider dependencies, and customer responsibilities;
- handling rules for logs, metadata, personal data, and regulated data;
- fees, renewal, termination, and any service credits; and
- exit, handoff, and unresolved-issue procedures.

If a term is not in the executed agreement, do not infer it from marketing copy,
a proposal, a community response, or the non-contractual security response
targets in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## Security incidents and vulnerabilities

Use the private routes in [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) for a suspected product
vulnerability, even if a commercial support relationship exists. Do not put
credentials, vault contents, cookies, device codes, or sensitive customer data
in email or a public issue. A support agreement cannot expand a third-party
provider's obligations or make an uncommissioned Phantom hosted service live.
