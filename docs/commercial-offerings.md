# Commercial offerings around the MIT core

Phantom's CLI, local vault, proxy, MCP server, and other repository source are
available under the [MIT License](../LICENSE). You do not need an enterprise
license, paid seat, or Ashlr AI account to exercise the rights that license
grants.

Ashlr AI can separately discuss paid evaluation, integration, and support work.
A commercial agreement buys only the services, deliverables, and commitments it
expressly names. It does not reduce the open-source rights already granted by
the MIT License.

## Current paths

| Path | Available boundary | Commercial requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Open-source use | Self-directed use, modification, and distribution of repository source under MIT; community support through GitHub. | None from Phantom beyond compliance with the license. |
| Enterprise evaluation | A reversible, non-production evaluation with named repositories, clients, owners, exclusions, and acceptance criteria. | Written scope, price, schedule, and terms before work begins. |
| Integration engineering | Environment-specific implementation or migration work around shipped local paths and supported clients. | Written deliverables, dependencies, acceptance, and change-control terms. |
| Commercial support | Defined advisory, implementation, or incident-assistance coverage for an agreed environment. | Response targets, hours, channels, exclusions, and fees exist only in the signed agreement. |
| Government evaluation | Evidence-first evaluation against a named public-sector environment and its technical or procurement requirements. | Written scope and terms; no contract vehicle or authorization is implied. |

Use the [enterprise adoption guide](enterprise-adoption.md) for the technical
evaluation process and [commercial support](commercial-support.md) for the
support boundary.

## Not offered as a current capability

This repository and website do not represent any of the following as available:

- a generally available Phantom Cloud service or hosted control plane;
- a supported self-hosted enterprise control plane;
- shipped SSO, SAML, or SCIM integration;
- SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP, FIPS, CMMC, HIPAA, PCI DSS, or another
  certification, validation, authorization, or attestation;
- a government contract vehicle or procurement schedule;
- a contractual SLA outside an executed agreement; or
- completed production, provider, security-review, or customer acceptance.

Product work for a missing control can be discussed as proposed work. A
proposal is not implementation; source implementation is not deployment;
deployment is not provider commissioning, certification, authorization, or
customer acceptance.

## Prepare an evaluation request

Email [mason@ashlr.ai](mailto:mason@ashlr.ai) with:

1. the repositories, teams, operating systems, and AI clients in scope;
2. the exact local workflow and non-production acceptance criteria;
3. security, data-residency, procurement, and support requirements;
4. prohibited actions and approval owners;
5. the required timeline and decision owner; and
6. any contractual prerequisite that must be resolved before technical work.

Do not send secrets, access tokens, vault contents, customer data, controlled
unclassified information, or production credentials in the initial request.
Commercial availability is established only by an executed agreement, not by
this page or an email exchange.
